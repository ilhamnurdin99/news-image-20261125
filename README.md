A newspaper front page summing up Wednesday, 25 November 2026 in one image:
36 dated stories across 9 regions — the US tells its citizens to leave
Iran after Iranian drones target US bases in Bahrain and Kuwait, two more
tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz, all Cartagena hostages are freed,
and a flooded gold mine in Bogor leaves 9 dead with 18 still trapped. Every
story happened, or was first reported, on 25 November 2026 (UTC); the
DATE CHECK box lists the neighbouring days' big news that was left out.
Unlike the 24 Nov edition, every brief number (14–36) is coloured by its
story's region, using the same palette as the region bar.

`main.ts` copies the front page (`entry.png`, 1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to the
grader's output directory as `entry.png`. The page was composed in HTML/CSS
and every illustration (including the world map with numbered pins) was drawn
in code — no photos.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
```
